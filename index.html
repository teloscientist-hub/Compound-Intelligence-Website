How it works: four stages of AI use

Each stage builds on the one before it. Most businesses are still at stage one. We take you to stage four.

  1. Stage 1

    Prompting

    Drafts, summaries and quick answers. Useful, but the AI forgets everything between sessions.

  2. Stage 2

    Context

    The AI is given your brand voice, messaging, expertise and audience. This is the start of a Brand Brain.

  3. Stage 3

    Agents

    AI that does real work: researching markets, producing content, running and analyzing campaigns.

  4. Stage 4

    One system

    All agents share one intelligence. Every new capability inherits what the system already knows.

Your intelligence stays yours.
